Privacy policy

“PDF Page Organizer — Merge, Split, Rotate” extension. Last updated: 1 November 2026.

In one line

This extension does not collect, transmit or store any personal data. Your files never leave your computer.

Your documents

The PDFs and images you open are processed inside the browser, in your computer's memory. They are not uploaded to any server, ours or anyone else's. When you close the tab, nothing is left behind. The extension works with the connection switched off.

What is saved on your computer

  • the licence state (trial, Pro, free) and the installation date;
  • your working preferences (page size, image quality);
  • whether you have already seen the review request.

They are kept in the browser's local storage and stay on your computer.

Payment

Buying the Pro version is handled by ExtensionPay, which relies on Stripe. If you decide to buy, your email address and payment details are handled by them, not by us: we never see or store your card details. The extension talks to extensionpay.com only to find out whether the licence is active. Their policies: extensionpay.com/privacy and stripe.com/privacy.

What we do not do

  • no usage statistics, no analytics service;
  • no advertising, no advertising profile;
  • no permissions on the sites you visit: the extension cannot read the pages you open;
  • no selling or sharing of data with third parties, because we have none.

Contact

For any question about this policy, write to the address shown on the extension's listing in the Chrome Web Store.
